THE FOUR QUESTIONS
------------------
1. How many days a week can staff work from home, and who approves it?
   Up to 2 days a week, approved by your line manager.
   Source: Remote-Work Guide, section 2. New employees work in the office
   every day for their first 3 months (also section 2). Exceptions longer
   than four weeks also need the People Team.
   Watch for: "3 days." That was the old rule in version 1.3, mentioned in
   section 7 of the guide as what changed.

2. Can I claim a taxi home after a client dinner that ends at 10:00 p.m.?
   Yes. A client dinner counts as a work event, and a taxi or rideshare
   home can be claimed when a work event ends after 9:00 p.m., up to
   US$60 one way, with the receipt and the name of the event.
   Source: Expenses Policy, section 4. The dinner itself needs your line
   manager's approval beforehand (section 5).

3. When does dental coverage start for a new employee?
   On the first day of the month after you complete 60 days of
   employment. Source: Benefits Summary, section 1 (the table and the
   worked example). Medical coverage starts on day one, which is an easy
   mix-up.

4. What's the company's policy on bringing pets to the office?
   None of the documents mention pets. A good answer says the documents
   don't cover it. Any rule about pets came from somewhere else.


CONFLICTS BETWEEN DOCUMENTS (for What to ask next)
--------------------------------------------------
- Mileage rate: Travel Policy section 4 says US$0.62 per mile. Expenses
  Policy section 6 says US$0.58 per mile.
- Receipts: Travel Policy section 6 says keep receipts for costs of US$75
  or more. Expenses Policy section 3 says attach a receipt for costs of
  US$25 or more.
The Travel Policy is older (July 2026) than the Expenses Policy (January
2027). The Staff Handbook says the more specific document applies, but it
doesn't say which wins between two specific documents.

- Whether you can bring a guest to the office for lunch.
