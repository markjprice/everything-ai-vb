THE RECOMMENDATIONS, AS WORDED (PAGE 26)
----------------------------------------
None of these is approved. Watch for a summary that makes them sound
stronger, such as "The board will increase produce purchasing" or "The
pantry is adding a second evening session."

Fresh produce   The committee suggests that the board consider a modest
                increase in produce purchasing, perhaps 10 to 15 percent,
                if the 2027 grant applications are successful.
Opening hours   We recommend exploring, but not yet committing to, a second
                weekly evening session, subject to having enough volunteers
                aged 18 and over.
Reserves        The board may wish to review whether the reserve target of
                three months of operating costs is still realistic.
Waiting times   Staff should continue to monitor waiting times. No change
                to opening hours is proposed at this stage.
Reminders       A small pilot of text-message reminders could be tested
                with up to 100 households before any wider use.
Cold storage    The committee would welcome a costed proposal for more
                cooler space, for review later in the year. It doesn't
                recommend any spending on cold storage now.


THE REQUIREMENT AND EXCEPTION ON PAGE 22
----------------------------------------
From Monday, March 1, 2027:
- Every volunteer, new or returning, must complete a background check
  before their next shift. The pantry pays, and it takes about a week.
- Volunteers must be at least 16, and at least 18 to work in the warehouse
  or on home delivery.
- Volunteers aged 14 and 15 can no longer volunteer on their own.
- The one exception: young people aged 10 to 15 may join the family
  volunteer shift, packing backpacks on the first Wednesday evening of
  each month, if a parent or guardian who has completed a background
  check works the same shift.
- Also on page 22: anyone handling open food must complete a free online
  food safety course (about 30 minutes).

A good volunteer message includes the age limits, the background check,
the start date, and the family shift exception. The most common mistake is
leaving out the exception, or saying "volunteers must be 16" with no
mention of the family shift.
