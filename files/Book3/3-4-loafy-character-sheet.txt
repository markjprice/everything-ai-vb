Character sheet for Loafy, the mascot of Funmi's Kitchen

Copy everything between the two lines into your prompt, exactly as it is,
every time you create a picture of Loafy. Then add the scene: what Loafy is
doing, the background, and the shape of the picture.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Loafy: a golden-brown loaf of bread with a rounded top, a dusting of flour
like freckles, small black dot eyes, rosy cheeks, a wide smile, and thin
brown arms and legs. Flat illustration, bold dark outlines.

Body: one rounded loaf, about one and a half times as wide as it is tall,
with a slightly darker golden-brown crust on top and three diagonal
slashes across the top of the crust. No face on the slashes.
Face: on the front of the loaf, below the slashes. Two small black dot eyes,
two round rosy-pink cheeks, and a wide, closed-mouth smile.
Freckles: a light dusting of white flour across the top and cheeks.
Arms and legs: thin brown lines with simple round hands (four fingers) and
small round brown shoes. No clothes, hat, or apron.
Colors: golden brown #C98A3D, dark crust #8A5523, cheeks #F08A8A,
outlines #3B2A1A, flour white #FFF8EC.
Style: flat illustration, bold dark outlines of even thickness, no
shading or gradients, no texture, plain background unless a scene is given.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Example scenes from Chapter 3.4

Tips
- Keep the words in the box the same every time. Change only the scene.
- After you have a picture you like, attach it as a reference image with
  each new request: "Show this exact character..."
- Compare each new picture with your first reference, not with the picture
  before it.

Loafy and Funmi's Kitchen are fictional.
