THE PRICE SIGN
--------------
The small sign reads:
  Cinnamon rolls
  $3.75 each

The sign is small, so an assistant may misread the price, guess a
different price, or say it can't read the sign. Saying it can't read the
sign is an honest answer. Giving a confident wrong price is a mistake.


EASY MISTAKES TO WATCH FOR
--------------------------
- Counting the blueberry and chocolate chip muffins as one group of 11,
  or mixing up which is which. They have the same white paper cases.
- Miscounting the cookies, because the back row is partly hidden behind
  the front row.
- Calling all 5 bagels the same kind. Two have sesame seeds.
- Calling the bagels donuts.
- Miscounting the croissants or cinnamon rolls by one.
