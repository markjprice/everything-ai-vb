6-3-real-or-ai: PICTURES STILL NEEDED

This folder should hold ten pictures for the Chapter 6.3 task: five real
photos and five AI-generated pictures, numbered 01.jpg to 10.jpg in a mixed
order, plus answer-key.txt.

These couldn't be created in the session that made the other sample files:
- Real photos must come from a camera, or from a source that allows reuse,
  such as your own photos or public-domain collections (for example, the
  Library of Congress or Wikimedia Commons, checking each license).
- AI-generated pictures must come from an image generator, using a tool
  whose terms allow you to publish them.

Suggestions so the test is fair and useful:
- Use the same subjects for both groups, so subject matter isn't a clue:
  for example, a kitchen, a street scene, a dog, a plate of food, and a
  portrait of a fictional person (AI) or a consenting adult (real).
- Avoid real, identifiable people in AI pictures, and get permission from
  anyone identifiable in a real photo.
- Make the AI pictures realistic. Include at least one with a subtle flaw
  (hands, text, reflections) and at least one with no visible flaw.
- Remove metadata from all ten files, so file details don't give the answer.
  Keep the originals, with any Content Credentials, separately if you want
  readers to try the Content Credentials checker on one of them.
- Use the same size and format (JPG, about 1600 pixels wide) for all ten.

Then fill in answer-key.txt and delete this README.
