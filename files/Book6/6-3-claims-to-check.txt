Book 6, Chapter 6.2: Checking AI's Answers
Five claims to check

These five claims come from a fictional AI answer to the question "Give me
five interesting facts about history, science, and money that I could use
in a short talk." Check each one with the three-question fact-check before
you decide whether you'd use it. The answer key is in a separate file,
6-2-claims-answer-key.txt. Try not to look at it until you've finished.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

CLAIM 1 (history)
The Wright brothers made the first powered, controlled airplane flights on
December 17, 1903, near Kitty Hawk, North Carolina.

CLAIM 2 (science)
At sea level, pure water boils at 100 degrees Celsius, which is 212 degrees
Fahrenheit.

CLAIM 3 (money)
In the United States, you can put up to $6,000 a year into an individual
retirement account (IRA), or $7,000 if you're 50 or older.

CLAIM 4 (science)
Mount Everest, the highest mountain above sea level, is 8,489 meters
(27,851 feet) tall.

CLAIM 5 (history)
In his inaugural address in January 1961, President John F. Kennedy said:
"Let us never tire of building bridges, for every bridge we build brings us
closer to the stars."

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

For each claim, note:
  How costly would it be if it were wrong?  Low / High
  How likely is it to be wrong?             Lower / Higher
  Where does it come from?
  Independent source that agrees (or disagrees):
  Does it make sense, and is it current?
  Verdict:  Use  /  Don't use  /  Use with changes
