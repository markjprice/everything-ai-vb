A good easy-to-read version includes all of these:

DEADLINES
1. Send back Form HEA-R2, filled in and signed, so it arrives by
   Friday, February 26, 2027.
2. Send proof of your household income (from the last 30 days) so it
   arrives by Friday, March 12, 2027. Write your case number on each page.

THE AMOUNT
- Your payment should go up from US$142 to US$156 a month, starting with
  the April 2027 payment, if your benefit continues. You don't need to do
  anything for this.

THE CONDITION IN THE THIRD PARAGRAPH
- You must also take part in a short phone interview. The office will write
  to you with the date and time after it gets your form.
- If you miss the interview and don't call to change it before the time,
  your case will be closed, even if you sent the form and documents on time.

OTHER DETAILS WORTH KEEPING
- Your current benefit period ends on Wednesday, March 31, 2027.
- Case number: R4-HEA-0638217.
- Helpline: (614) 555-0170, Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. You
  can ask for help with the form, or for the letter in large print or
  another language.
- You can ask for a fair hearing if you disagree with a decision.

The most common mistake is leaving out the phone interview, because the
letter mentions it in the middle of a long sentence.

The agency, person, and benefit are fictional.
