THE HIDDEN FAULT
Orlo Glide 200 has the highest rating, but its 1-star and 2-star reviews
share a pattern: dog and cat hair wraps around the bristle brush, and the
side brush motor fails after about 3 months. Support says hair damage
isn't covered. The store's AI review summary only says "a few customers
mention issues with hair."

THE SPONSORED PRODUCT
Zentra PetPro S is a paid, sponsored listing. Its price is only under
US$300 during a sale that "ends Sunday," and much of the price pays for a
self-emptying base Dana doesn't need. Its low-star reviews mention a loud
base, the cost of replacement bags, and a base that stopped working.

A REASONABLE CHOICE FOR DANA
Dustwise R5 Pet. It meets every must-have: under US$300, made for pet hair
with a tangle-free roller, works on hard floors and rugs, and no base to
pay for. Trade-offs: louder, a smaller bin, camera navigation that
struggles in dark rooms, and occasional app disconnections. It has the
longest warranty, and reviews from owners of 9 to 13 months are positive.

Other choices can be reasonable if the reasons are clear, but a choice of
Orlo Glide 200 should explain how Dana will handle the hair problem.

All products, reviews, and the store are fictional.
