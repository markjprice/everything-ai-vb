JUNIOR DATA ANALYST
Brightwater Insurance Group  |  Columbus, Ohio (hybrid: 3 days in the office)
Full time  |  US$54,000 to US$60,000 a year, depending on experience
Closing date: Friday, February 26, 2027

ABOUT US
Brightwater Insurance Group provides home and car insurance to about 200,000
customers in Ohio, Indiana, and Kentucky. Our Customer Insights team turns
data about claims, calls, and surveys into answers that help us serve
customers better.

ABOUT THE ROLE
You'll join a team of five analysts. You'll prepare weekly reports, clean and
check data, build simple dashboards, and help answer questions from our
claims and customer service teams. You'll learn from experienced analysts,
and we'll support you to gain a professional certification in your first
year.

WHAT YOU'LL DO
- Prepare weekly and monthly reports on claims, calls, and customer
  satisfaction.
- Clean and combine data from different systems, and check it for errors.
- Build and update dashboards in Power BI.
- Write short summaries of what the numbers show, for people who aren't
  analysts.
- Answer data questions from other teams, and explain your method.
- Help improve how the team uses AI tools for routine reporting, with
  careful checks on every figure.

WHAT YOU'LL NEED
- A degree in a subject that involved working with numbers, such as math,
  economics, statistics, business, psychology, or a science, or equivalent
  experience.
- Confidence with Excel or Google Sheets, including formulas, pivot tables,
  and charts.
- Some experience with SQL or Python, from a course, project, or job.
- Clear writing, and the ability to explain numbers in plain English.
- Attention to detail. You check your own work before anyone else sees it.

NICE TO HAVE
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau.
- Experience of customer service or a customer-facing job.
- An example of a project where you used data to answer a real question.

WHAT WE OFFER
- 22 days of vacation, plus public holidays.
- Medical, dental, and vision insurance.
- 401(k) with a 5% company match.
- US$1,500 a year for training and certifications.
- A mentor for your first year.

HOW TO APPLY
Send your CV or résumé and a short cover letter (no more than one page) to
careers@brightwater.example, with "Junior Data Analyst" in the subject line.
Tell us about one time you used data to answer a question or solve a problem.
