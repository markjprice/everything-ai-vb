The question in the task:
  Can I park here on Saturday at 3:00 p.m. for two hours without a permit?

THE SHORT ANSWER
----------------
No. You can park at 3:00 p.m., but you must move the car by 4:00 p.m.
That's one hour, not two.


WHAT THE SIGN SAYS, PANEL BY PANEL
----------------------------------
Panel 1 (red):    NO PARKING  7 AM - 9 AM  MON - FRI
Panel 2 (green):  2 HOUR PARKING  9 AM - 4 PM  EXCEPT SUNDAY
Panel 3 (green):  AREA 4 PERMIT HOLDERS EXEMPT FROM TIME LIMIT
Panel 4 (red):    TOW-AWAY ZONE  NO STOPPING  4 PM - 7 PM  EXCEPT SUNDAY


HOW THE RULES WORK TOGETHER ON SATURDAY FROM 3:00 TO 5:00 P.M.
----------------------------------------------------------------
Panel 1  Doesn't apply. It covers Monday to Friday mornings only.
Panel 2  Applies. "Except Sunday" means Monday to Saturday, so the 2-hour
         limit is in force on Saturday from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
         Arriving at 3:00 p.m. is allowed.
Panel 3  Doesn't help. You have no permit, and the permit only removes
         the time limit. It doesn't allow stopping in a tow-away period.
Panel 4  This is the line that gives the answer. "Except Sunday" means it
         applies on Saturday, so no stopping is allowed from 4:00 p.m. to
         7:00 p.m. A car still there after 4:00 p.m. can be towed.


MISTAKES TO WATCH FOR
---------------------
- Reading "Except Sunday" as "weekdays only" and saying panel 4 doesn't
  apply on Saturday.
- Saying "Yes, two hours is within the 2-hour limit" without checking
  panel 4.
- Applying panel 1 to Saturday.
- Saying the Area 4 permit allows parking after 4:00 p.m. It doesn't.
- Misreading the small print on panel 3.


This sign is fictional. Parking rules vary by city, so always read the
actual sign where you park.
